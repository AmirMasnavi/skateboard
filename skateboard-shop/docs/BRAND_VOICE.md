# Mellow Skateboard Brand Voice & Content

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** Mellow Skateboard
- **Slogan:** "Stay Mellow"
- **Mission:** A community-driven brand focused on the authentic love of skating. For the community, by the community.
- **Vibe:** Clean, minimalist, urban, and welcoming. Not just a shop, but a movement.

## Core Content for Pages

### 1. About Section (Hero/Home)
- **Title:** The Mellow Story
- **Content:** Born from the streets and built for the community. Mellow isn't just about the gear; it's about the ride, the culture, and the people who keep the spirit of skateboarding alive. We provide premium decks and apparel for those who live to stay mellow.

### 2. Shop Page
- **Headline:** Full Collection
- **Subheadline:** Premium hardware and soft goods curated for quality and performance.

### 3. New Arrivals Page
- **Headline:** Fresh Off The Truck
- **Subheadline:** The latest drops from Mellow and our favorite partners. Stay ahead of the curve.

### 4. Brands Page
- **Headline:** Our Partners
- **Content:** We curate only the best. From heritage giants like Santa Cruz and Powell-Peralta to independent local shapers.

### 5. Social Presence
- **Instagram Handle:** @mellow.skateboard
- **Link:** [https://www.instagram.com/mellow.skateboard/](https://www.instagram.com/mellow.skateboard/)

## Tone of Voice Guidelines
- **Keep it "Mellow":** Avoid aggressive sales language. Use "Discover," "Explore," and "Join us."
- **Focus on Community:** Use "We" and "Our community."
- **Typography Style:** All-caps for impact headings, clean sans-serif for story-telling.
